## 📝 Form Copy & Paste Details (When Submission Form Opens)

### 📌 Project Title
**JanMitra AI — India's Multi-Agent Citizen Co-Pilot Platform**

### 📌 Tagline / Short Description
An intelligent, voice-enabled multi-agent AI platform empowering 1.4 billion Indian citizens with 22-language access to government schemes, healthcare, scholarships, agriculture, employment, legal guidance, and emergency SOS assistance.

### 📌 Problem Addressed
Over **₹1.5 Lakh Crore in government scheme benefits** remain unclaimed annually in India due to complex paperwork, language barriers, fragmented state portals, and lack of guidance for rural citizens.

### 📌 Solution & Core Capabilities
1. **7 Specialized AI Agents**: Government Schemes, Healthcare, Education, Agriculture, Employment, Legal, and Emergency SOS coordinated via a LangGraph Intent Router.
2. **Vision AI Document OCR**: Upload physical certificates (Aadhaar, Income Certificate, Farmer Records) for instant OCR parsing and scheme matching using Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision.
3. **Multilingual Voice Co-Pilot & 3D Avatar ("Janvi")**: Interactive voice-to-voice communication across 22 official Indian languages.
4. **Citizen Digital Portal & Journey Timeline**: Interactive progress tracking, goal checklist, verified document vault, and 1-click Action Plan PDF generation.
5. **Privacy & Security Centre**: Designed with DPDP Act 2023 principles, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and task-bound ephemeral file processing.
