# Problem Statement - JanMitra AI

## Background
Millions of Indian citizens struggle to find, understand, and apply for government schemes, educational aid, agricultural subsidies, and healthcare benefits due to fragmented information portals, complex language, and bureaucracy.

## Solution
JanMitra AI provides a single conversational interface powered by a multi-agent AI system. Citizens can ask questions in natural language, receive verified guidance, and get personalized action plans.
